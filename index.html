Chip Fire

A puzzle game built on real mathematics. Tap an orb to fire it: it sends one chip along each of its edges. Clear every board in as few moves as you can.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

The game

Every board is a graph: orbs joined by edges, each orb holding a number. Fire orbs to move chips around until every orb is cleared. The game always knows the fewest moves possible, so you can chase a perfect score.

Five worlds

Each world plays by different arithmetic, from whole numbers to numbers that wrap around.

The math behind it

Chip-firing is a simple rule that mathematicians have studied for decades. It connects combinatorics, graph theory, algebra, statistical physics (as the sandpile model of avalanches) and algebraic geometry (through a Riemann–Roch theorem for graphs). Many questions about it are still unsolved.

  • Firing order never changes where a game ends up, so only how many times each orb fires matters.
  • Every graph has a hidden group whose size is its number of spanning trees.
  • In the whole-number world, a board can always be cleared once its chips add up to at least edges − orbs + 1.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Chip Fire has no accounts and keeps no copy of your progress.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

In the game, open Records & options and choose “Move progress to another device.” Copy your progress code on the old device and paste it on the new one.

I bought the full game. How do I get it on a new device?

Open the mode list in the game and tap “Restore purchases,” signed in with the same Apple or Google account you bought it with.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. Every puzzle, including the daily puzzle and boards friends share with you, works without an internet connection.

Can I use Chip Fire in a class?

Sure. Build a board in the game, tap Share, and give your students the code. Everyone gets the same board, and the game shows the fewest moves possible.
