Privacy policy

Effective October 10, 2026

In short: Chip Fire does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device.

This policy explains how the Chip Fire game for iPhone, iPad and Android (“the app”) and this website handle information. Chip Fire is made by an independent developer (“I”, “me”), who can be reached at the email address at the end of this page.

Information the app keeps on your device

To save your game, the app stores the following on your device only:

  • your progress, scores, stars and settings;
  • boards you build in the game;
  • the name you choose to type for your scorecards, if you type one.

This information is never sent to me or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising identifiers. It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking software, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Sharing scorecards and boards

When you tap Share, the app prepares a message containing your scores, the boards you played or built, and the name you typed for your scorecards, if any. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send that message yourself, and you decide who receives it. The message and its code are handled by whatever app you use to send it.

Purchases

If you buy the full game, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple’s App Store or Google Play. I do not receive your payment details or your account information. The store keeps a record of your purchase so that you can restore it on another device.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily puzzle reminder, the reminder is scheduled on your device. No information is sent to a server to deliver it, and you can turn it off at any time in the game or in your device settings.

Children

Chip Fire is suitable for all ages. Because the app does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles information, I will update this page and change the date at the top before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy or anything else: chipfire.game@gmail.com.
